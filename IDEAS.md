- **[2026-08-02 16:30]** [#verification] Verification ledger — the missing artifact. The highest-value output of a working session is the corrections: claims that were checked and did not survive. `spell-close-session` captures what happened plus sanitized lessons learned; it throws away what was asserted, what was checked, what the check showed, and what changed. Those corrections are the evidence base for pattern findings — EF-21 and EF-24 both exist only because someone noticed the pattern across them — they are measurable as a calibration rate for the workflow, and they are how a new session inherits the behaviour rather than the personality. Proposal: a spell that extracts a verification ledger from a session on demand, separate from close-session. Confirmed instances from batch 001 alone: a 23-versus-6 file count stated confidently and used to justify a commit message; “all three pipeline templates” when there were four; EF-21's premise contradicted by its own source; fabricated Model and author trailers surviving eight PRs and human review; a dedup that deleted a lesson it assumed was duplicated; an operator's own "eight worktrees, seven prunable" claim, checked against the actual owning environment and found false — the worktrees were healthy, and the false read traced to a cross-filesystem mount rather than dishonesty on either side (see EF-33). Grading probe for whether a session inherited the protocol: “What does `Role: developer` in our commit trailers resolve from?” Plausible answer “the agent roster” is wrong; correct behaviour is to check, find no `.arcane/agents.yaml`, and report it is an example value at `git-conventions.md` line 554 that was never sourced. <!-- status: new -->
