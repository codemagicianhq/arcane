Sign-in result pages

spell login shows one of the three sign-in-callback-*.html pages in the
browser when a sign-in ends: success, denied (the sign-in was cancelled) or
failure. They are compiled output of arcane-ui, Code Magician LLC's design
system, copied here without changes.

Design and artwork: Copyright (c) 2026 Code Magician LLC. The pages, including
the Gate artwork inlined in them, are distributed with arcane-cli for use with
Arcane. The arcane-ui source and the artwork's masters are not part of this
package.

Fonts: each page embeds Latin subsets of Chakra Petch (Medium and SemiBold)
and Rajdhani (Medium and SemiBold), unmodified, under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their copyright notices and licence texts are in
OFL-chakra-petch.txt and OFL-rajdhani.txt.
