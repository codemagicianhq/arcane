# Idea Inbox — {Venture Name}

Per-venture idea book (hub-side). Add with `spell-save-idea --venture {slug}` (or natural
phrasing, e.g. "save this idea for {slug}"); triage with `spell-todo --sweep`; promote downstream
with `spell-manifest`. Entries are append-only; mark status in the trailing comment rather than
deleting.

Private by default, including for ventures whose own repo is public — promotion to a public
destination is a deliberate disclosure event, not a side effect of saving an idea here.
