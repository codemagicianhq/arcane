## Claim

Arcane models code under language conventions and multiple ventures under `{BUSINESS_ROOT}`, but it cannot describe one repository whose contents represent one non-business subject.

## Verification

Confirmed. `{BUSINESS_ROOT}` is explicitly a directory of per-business folders in [spell-bootstrap-business.prompt.md](../../../src/assets/.github/prompts/spell-bootstrap-business.prompt.md#L32-L36) and [spell-present-arcane.prompt.md](../../../src/assets/.github/prompts/spell-present-arcane.prompt.md#L35-L44). Repository-wide source search found no `{SUBJECT_ROOT}` or single-subject model. The venture template also tells users to copy into `ventures/<name>/` at [overview.md](../../../src/assets/.arcane/templates/venture-template/overview.md#L12).

## Impact

A repository already named for one subject is pushed toward a redundant portfolio wrapper or left with framework files intermixed with subject matter at the root.

## Proposed fix

Add a subject-shape decision to initialization: one subject, a portfolio, or mixed. Define `{SUBJECT_ROOT}` independently from `{BUSINESS_ROOT}` and let profile defaults suggest, not force, a directory name.

## Open questions

May subject and business roots coexist? Is root-as-subject a supported value, or must Arcane always separate framework and subject trees physically?
