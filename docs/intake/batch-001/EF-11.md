## Claim

Arcane provides no records-oriented convention for retaining superseded material and linking it to its replacement.

## Verification

Confirmed as a missing convention. The docs-only section of [cicd-standards.md](../../../src/assets/.arcane/governance/cicd-standards.md#L49-L52) addresses Git flow only. Repository-wide searches of canonical prompts and governance found no record tombstone, supersession, or records quarantine pattern. The only `superseded` Git guidance concerns closing pull requests at [git-conventions.md](../../../src/assets/.arcane/governance/git-conventions.md#L507), not documents.

## Impact

Records repositories must choose ad hoc between deletion, ambiguous archives, and duplicate live files. That weakens discoverability and retention evidence.

## Proposed fix

Define a profile-level supersession convention with location, naming, replacement links, retention metadata, and rules for when deletion is prohibited or separately approved.

## Open questions

Should superseded records move under each subject, remain in place with tombstone metadata, or follow an external retention system? Regulatory requirements may override one universal layout.
