## Claim

Distributed governance relies on ADR-048 for docs-only Git policy, but consuming repositories receive no copy of that decision or its rationale.

## Verification

Confirmed. [cicd-standards.md](../../../src/assets/.arcane/governance/cicd-standards.md#L38-L52) cites ADR-048 twice for code-versus-docs branch policy, and [agent-output.instructions.md](../../../src/assets/.github/instructions/agent-output.instructions.md#L23-L25) cites it for merge strategy. The distributed continuity template [DECISIONS.md](../../../src/assets/DECISIONS.md#L1-L17) is an empty repository-local ADR log and contains no framework decisions. The consumer therefore cannot resolve ADR-048 from installed assets.

The batch understates the broader drift: the CI/CD executive summary also cites ADR-049 and ADR-051 at [cicd-standards.md](../../../src/assets/.arcane/governance/cicd-standards.md#L11-L17), which are likewise not included in the empty decision template.

## Impact

Operators see settled-looking citations without access to the reasoning, especially when the cited policy conflicts with an executing spell as in EF-19.

This batch demonstrated the concrete harm. [cicd-standards.md](../../../src/assets/.arcane/governance/cicd-standards.md#L49-L52) says docs-only repositories may merge locally without a PR, while [spell-close-session.prompt.md](../../../src/assets/.github/prompts/spell-close-session.prompt.md#L103-L127) hardcodes the full branch, Azure DevOps PR, and remote-update lifecycle. ADR-048 was the one cited source that could have resolved which behavior expressed the intended decision, and the consumer could not read it. An unresolvable citation is worse than no citation: it looks settled and authoritative while withholding the reasoning needed to resolve a live contradiction.

## Proposed fix

Separate framework decision references from consumer-local `DECISIONS.md`. Either distribute a curated framework decision record with stable links, or inline sufficient rationale and replace inaccessible IDs with public canonical URLs.

Make recurrence prevention mechanical. Add a build-time or `doctor` check that extracts every `ADR-NNN` reference from distributed governance, prompts, and instructions and verifies that each resolves to a decision shipped in the package. Treat unresolved IDs like broken links and fail the applicable quality gate with the referring file and line. Include fixtures for resolved, missing, and malformed references. This check must cover at least ADR-048, ADR-049, and ADR-051, which already demonstrate recurrence across the current distributed material.

EF-23 overlaps with EF-24 but remains separate. A citation is a concrete promise that the reader can inspect the referenced reasoning; here that promise is unwired. EF-24 defines the general enforcement-contract classification, while EF-23 is one breach with a narrow, inexpensive resolution check.

## Open questions

Which historical ADRs are framework-owned and publishable, and should offline installs be able to resolve every normative citation without network access?
