## Claim

Arcane has no workflow for receiving de-identified findings from consuming repositories, verifying each against current source, obtaining item-level consent, and routing only accepted items.

## Verification

Confirmed as a feature gap. `spell-feedback` appends findings to the current repository's `FEEDBACK.md` and optionally hands substantial items to local TODO intake at [spell-feedback.prompt.md](../../../src/assets/.github/prompts/spell-feedback.prompt.md#L66-L99). `spell-save-idea` is deliberately immediate, local, and append-only at [spell-save-idea.prompt.md](../../../src/assets/.github/prompts/spell-save-idea.prompt.md#L7-L13) and [spell-save-idea.prompt.md](../../../src/assets/.github/prompts/spell-save-idea.prompt.md#L38-L57). The registered spell list contains no external intake workflow at [registry.ts](../../../src/modules/registry.ts#L97-L143).

## Impact

Cross-repository findings arrive through ad hoc documents without a standard boundary-redaction, version verification, consent, discard, or routing process. This batch itself is the first manual execution of the missing workflow.

The first execution produced 29 item reports in total, with one later deleted without trace under the discard rule. It handled EF-14 as one schema finding merged from three separate submissions, contradicted and re-scoped EF-21 instead of silently weakening it, exercised an item-level drop, and triggered a cross-cutting enforcement audit that surfaced five additional concrete bugs nobody had filed. Every verification, correction, consent decision, deletion, and routing dependency was performed manually because no Arcane spell produces this process. That operational burden and the quality improvements it produced are the evidence for the feature.

## Proposed fix

Specify `spell-intake` from the evidence produced by this dry run: receive, verify against current source, redact before writing, create one temporary report per item, survey keep/drop/defer, delete drops without a ledger, and route accepted items only after consent.

Do not build the spell after one execution. Run this intake process manually for at least one independent batch 002 before finalizing the PRD or implementation. One batch is an anecdote; the second distinguishes stable process requirements from accidents of this batch. Batch 001 already discovered an unanticipated fail-closed consent requirement and corrected one of its own premises. Assume batch 002 will change the design again, and capture those deltas before automating.

Like docs mode, `spell-intake` queues behind the concrete shipped-code defects EF-25 through EF-29, with EF-25 first. Research and a second manual run may proceed, but implementation must not displace fixes for destructive update behavior or other defects in shipped workflows.

## Open questions

Where do deferred reports live, how is source-version provenance represented, and what automated redaction checks can reject unsafe evidence without weakening it? Which batch-001 steps remain unchanged in batch 002, and which should be explicitly rejected as batch-specific before the spell contract is accepted?
